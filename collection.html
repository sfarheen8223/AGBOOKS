The Power of Habit

Author: Charles Duhigg

Category: Self-Help / Psychology

The Power of Habit explores the science behind why habits exist and how they can be changed. Charles Duhigg introduces the Habit Loop—Cue, Routine, and Reward—and explains how understanding habits can improve productivity, health, business performance, and personal success.

Through engaging stories and scientific research, the book demonstrates how habits shape our daily lives and how small changes can lead to remarkable results.

Who Says Elephants Can't Dance?

Author: Louis V. Gerstner Jr.

Category: Management / Business Leadership

Who Says Elephants Can't Dance? is the inspiring story of how Louis V. Gerstner Jr. transformed IBM from a struggling company into one of the world's leading technology organizations.

Drawing from his experience as IBM's CEO, Gerstner shares valuable lessons on leadership, organizational change, corporate culture, strategy, and managing large enterprises during periods of dramatic transformation.
